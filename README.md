# ECommerce Sales Analysis using Excel
This analysis was conducted to understand **sales performance, customer behavior and product trends at Ferns & Petals (FNP),** a gifting company specializing in products for occasions such as Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine’s Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries. The dataset comprised order details, product information, customer data, and dates. Using **Excel pivot tables and dashboards**, the following key insights were uncovered:

### Overall Sales Performance
- **Total Orders:** 1,000
- **Total Revenue:** ₹35,20,984
- **Average Customer Spend:** ₹3,520.98
- FNP demonstrates a healthy average order value indicating customers are willing to spend significantly on gifts

### Monthly Sales Trends
- Sales peaked in **March, May, September and October** show the highest revenue, aligning with major festive periods like **Holi/Spring** and the build up to the major **holiday** season
- **June, July and December** are the lowest performing months, suggesting a significant drop in demand during the mid-year summer and post holiday winter

### Occasion Wise Revenue Contribution
- **Anniversary, Raksha Bandhan and Holi** generated the highest revenue
- **Birthday's, Diwali and Valentines Day** recorded comparatively lower sales

### Customer Spending Patterns
- With an average spend of **₹3,520.98 per customer** FNPs pricing and product mix appeal mid to premium range customers

### Regional Sales Performance
- **Imphal, Dhanbad, Kavali and Haridwar** record the highest number of orders
- Expanding delivery capacity and regional promotions in these cities could boost customer satisfaction and repeat sales

### Revenue by Category
- **Color, Soft Toys and Sweets** are the highest revenue generators
- Perishable and personalized items like **cakes and mugs** are popular and by expanding customization options might increase the revenue

### Business Recommendation
- **Underperorming Categories:** Explore repositioning for **Plants and Mugs** or bundling them with popular items
- **Regional Targeting:** Strengthen logistics in high demand cities while building awareness in underperforming ones
- **Customer Engagement:** Introduce loyalty rewards for repeat purchases to increase average order value
