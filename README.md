# ECommerce Sales Analysis using Excel
This analysis was conducted to understand **sales performance, customer behavior and product trends for Ferns & Petals (FNP),** a gifting company specializing in products for occasions such as Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine’s Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries. The dataset comprised order details, product information, customer data, and dates. Using **Excel pivot tables and dashboards**, the following key insights were developed:

### Overall Sales Performance
- **Total Orders:** 1,000
- **Total Revenue:** ₹35,20,984
- **Average Customer Spend:** ₹3,520.98
- FNP demonstrates a healthy average order value which indicates customers are willing to spend significantly on gifts

### Monthly Sales Trends
- Sales peaked in **March, May, September and October** aligning with major festive periods like **Holi/Spring** and the build up to the major **holiday** season
- **June, July and December** are the lowest performing months, suggesting a significant drop in demand during the mid-year summer and post holiday winter

### Occasion Wise Revenue Contribution
- **Anniversaries, Raksha Bandhan and Holi** generated the highest revenue
- **Birthdays, Diwali and Valentines Day** recorded comparatively lower sales

### Customer Spending Patterns
- With an average spend of **₹3,520.98 per customer** FNP's pricing and product mix appeal mid to premium range customers

### Regional Sales Performance
- **Imphal, Dhanbad, Kavali and Haridwar** record the highest number of orders
- Expanding delivery capacity and regional promotions in these cities could boost customer satisfaction and repeat sales

### Technical Methodology & Tools
- **Platform:** Microsoft Excel(2019+)
- **ETL & Data Transformation:** Utilized **Power Query** for initial data ingestion, cleaning, and custom column creation
- **Data Modeling:** Established a unified data model using **Pivot Tables** to structure and aggregate data, enabling dynamic filtering by Date, Occasion, and Product Category
- **Visualization:** Created 8 distinct charts to visualize trends across Time (Monthly Sales), Geography (Top Cities by Orders), and Product performance

### Business Recommendation
- **Underperorming Categories:** Explore repositioning for **Plants and Mugs** or bundling them with popular items
- **Regional Targeting:** Strengthen logistics in high demand cities while building awareness in underperforming ones
- **Customer Engagement:** Introduce loyalty rewards for repeat purchases to increase average order value

### Conclusion
The sales analysis reveals that **Ferns & Petals** has a **strong mid-premium customer** base with  revenue peaks during **cultural occasions**. To maximize growth, FNP should focus on optimizing delivery timelines, leveraging top-performing products, expanding in high-order cities and driving targeted promotions during peak festive months.
